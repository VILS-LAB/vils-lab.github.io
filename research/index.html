RESEARCH / VILS LAB

Looking closer.
Thinking further.

Research in low-light object detection, reinforcement learning, and language models for malware detection, supervised by Dr. Arash Abdi.

01

Vision in low light

Mohammad Hesam Ghasemi explores object detection in low-light conditions, with reinforcement learning guiding image enhancement. The research connects enhancement decisions with the needs of the detector.

02

Reinforcement learning

Hesam Soleymani studies offline-to-online reinforcement learning: the transition from learning with stored experience to adapting through interaction with an environment.

03

Language models & security

Mojtaba Asadi explores malware detection with large language models, focusing on how code and behavioral information can support the analysis of potentially malicious software.
